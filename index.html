Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Version 0.25.0

CRACK CITY is an offline, single-player game. No account is required. The game does not collect or share personal data; progress and settings stay on your device.

Game data and retention

Progress, scores, unlocked weapons and appearances, headquarters upgrades, vehicle restoration, team missions and settings are stored in the app's local data area. The game does not send these records to a server operated by the publisher.

Local records remain until app data is cleared. On Android, use Settings > Apps > CRACK CITY > Storage to clear app data; menu names vary by device. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app removes its local records. This also removes your game progress. Your operating system's backup and restore settings apply separately, and a restore may bring records back.

Advertising, purchases and permissions

This version contains no advertising, analytics, tracking, account or real-money in-app purchase SDKs. In-game resources are earned by playing. The game does not request access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photo library.

The Unity Android package includes internet permission. This version does not use it to transmit game saves or personal data. An internet connection is not required to play. The iOS version does not send data over the network.

Google Play, Android, App Store and iOS

Google Play and Android may process installation information, device backups and system crash reports under their own services and privacy policies. The publisher may view aggregate installation and crash statistics provided by Google Play. Apple may likewise process information through the App Store and iOS (including iCloud backup) under its own policies; aggregate statistics and crash reports from users who opt in to share them may be visible in App Store Connect.

Policy changes

If the game's data practices change, this policy, the Google Play Data safety declarations and the App Store privacy details will be updated. The revision date appears on this page.
